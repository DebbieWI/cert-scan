# Limitations (v1.0 DRAFT)

*Plain-language header: the honest list of what this table cannot tell you. The paper's discussion section is not allowed to claim anything this file rules out.*

1. **It measures rules, not skills or security.** A "required" cell means a law or rule makes cybersecurity training a condition of the operator certificate. It says nothing about the quality of that training, whether operators learn from it, or whether any water system is more secure.
2. **"No provision found" is not "none."** Each negative rests on one state-specific source per row (a renewal rule or program page) plus a nationwide search. A provision in a document we did not read, or adopted after the access date, would be missed.
3. **"Credited" is undercounted.** Certifying bodies approve individual courses for credit, often without publishing the approvals. A state coded `no_provision_found` may still award credit for cybersecurity courses. Only states where an official document names cybersecurity as eligible, or a published approval exists, are coded `credited_optional`.
4. **Exam content is inferred from published outlines, not from exams.** The exam column records whether the exam provider's published outline lists a cybersecurity task. It does not show how many questions address it, and WPI membership does not prove a state has adopted the 2025 outlines.
5. **Pennsylvania's course content is unconfirmed.** Pennsylvania's mandatory security course is coded `general_security_only` until the Department confirms whether it covers cybersecurity.
6. **The District of Columbia's status is unconfirmed.** No independent DC operator certification program was identified; confirmation from DC government is pending.
7. **The 2016 baseline covers drinking water only.** EPA 816-R-16-002 summarizes drinking water programs. There is no comparable national wastewater baseline.
8. **Effective dates mix instrument types.** Required rows carry the date a statute or regulation took effect. Credited rows carry the date of the guidance or approval, which may postdate when credit was first given.
9. **Point in time.** All sources were accessed on 2026-10-23 unless a row says otherwise. Several states have pending legislation or studies (Illinois HB 3576; Utah DEQ assessment due 2026-12-31).
10. **Automated reading was used to find candidate text.** Pages were scanned by script for cybersecurity terms and read where terms appeared. Scripted scans can miss content in images or scanned PDFs; rows where the source could not be read are marked.
